## Overview

**KCO title fetcher** is a browser extension that retrieves the title currently playing on YouTube or Niconico and sends it to KCO Relay on your local machine.
Together with Kancolle Stream Overlay, it displays the current BGM title on your stream.

<!-- i18n:features -->
## Features

Reads the current title from YouTube / Niconico pages and sends it to a relay at `localhost` / `127.0.0.1`.

You can also manage a queue of upcoming videos in the extension.
An option removes YouTube playlist and mix parameters to make individual videos easier to handle.

<!-- i18n:installation -->
## Setup

1. Download and extract the source code.
2. Open the extensions page in Chrome / Edge.
3. Enable **Developer mode**.
4. Choose **Load unpacked** and select the extension's folder.
5. Start KCO Relay and enable the BGM display in Kancolle Stream Overlay.

<!-- i18n:sites -->
## Supported sites

- YouTube
- Niconico

<!-- i18n:related -->
## Related tools

- **KCO Relay**: Receives and relays titles locally.
- **Kancolle Stream Overlay**: Displays the relayed BGM titles on your stream.
